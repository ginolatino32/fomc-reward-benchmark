# Executed plans and departures

The plan JSON files record extensions designed after the historical development sample was already explored. Their timestamps are execution records, not a retroactive claim of preregistration.

## Matched inputs and functional form

Executed as a bounded diagnostic: fixed quadratic positive/negative count features, independently processed selected passages, phrase counts versus category votes, and common volume controls. All variants are retained, not filtered by their errors.

## New original-paper comparator

The ability to recover published vector paths made a direct comparator available during this turn. `AUTHOR_COMPARATOR_PLAN.json` was written before viewing its downstream comparison. The resulting counts are the authors' published human-coded aggregate observations, not our reconstruction and not new human annotation. A separately acquired appendix supplies an independent dated check and approximate original returns. `ADDITIONAL_SOURCE_DIAGNOSTICS.json` declares the later numerical-resolution and initial-training-length checks; these are post-discovery diagnostics, not confirmation.

## Return construction

The intended fresh French daily-return acquisition was attempted but did not complete in the runtime. We did not silently replace it with fabricated observations. The executed alternatives are (a) authors' graph-recovered original returns on their publication period, and (b) new formulas applied to the inherited genuine daily price cache. The latter are gross price returns, not a newly acquired French excess-total-return series. Full daily inclusion/exclusion ledgers accompany them.

## Uninspected sample

The official release calendar was inspected and 13 public minutes documents through July 2026 were identified. Their direct downloads failed in the runtime. More importantly, no authenticated interface for new same-model Gemini/Flash outputs was available. Neither a new holdout nor future observations are claimed. The existing broad historical sample remains explored.

## Source and model authentication

The downloaded author appendix has an exact recorded hash. Inherited embeddings and labels are identified by their supplied manifests and provider responses; offline numerical reproduction does not independently authenticate an external vendor invocation. No model was replaced merely because a new endpoint might be easier to access.
