- The observations in `data/author_*` and `data/figure5_*` were recovered from figures published by Anna Cieslak and Annette Vissing-Jorgensen (NBER Working Paper 26894, 2020; *Review of Financial Studies* 34(9), 2021). Please cite their work when you use them.
- Embeddings and tone labels were produced by Google Gemini models and are subject to the provider's terms of service.
- S&P 500 price data are not redistributed here. See `scripts/fetch_market_data.py`.
